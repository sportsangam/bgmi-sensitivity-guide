# BGMI Sensitivity Guide

A practical guide to understanding and adjusting sensitivity settings in Battlegrounds Mobile India (BGMI).

Sensitivity is one of the first things many players change when trying to improve their aim. However, copying a setting from another player does not always produce the same result. Your device, screen refresh rate, gyro response, controls, and playing style can all affect how a sensitivity setting feels.

## Camera Sensitivity

Camera sensitivity controls how quickly the camera moves when you swipe the screen without firing.

Higher values can make it easier to turn quickly and track moving players, while lower values can feel more controlled for players who prefer slower movements.

There is no single camera sensitivity that works perfectly for everyone. Start with a comfortable value and make small changes rather than changing several settings at once.

## ADS Sensitivity

ADS sensitivity affects camera movement while aiming down sights.

A useful approach is to test each scope separately. If your crosshair moves too far when making small adjustments, try lowering the sensitivity. If tracking feels slow, increase it gradually.

## Gyroscope Sensitivity

Gyroscope sensitivity controls how much the camera moves when you physically tilt your device.

Players who use gyro often rely on it for recoil control and small aiming adjustments. Higher gyro values can make fast corrections easier, but they can also feel difficult to control when you are not used to them.

For new gyro players, starting with moderate values and increasing them over time is usually easier than immediately copying a professional player's settings.

## Choosing Sensitivity for Your Device

The same sensitivity can feel different on different phones.

Things that can affect the experience include:

* Screen refresh rate
* Touch response
* Device performance
* Gyroscope response
* Screen size
* FPS stability
* Your control layout

For this reason, professional player settings are better treated as a starting point rather than a universal formula.

## How to Test a New Sensitivity

When changing your settings, test them in Training Ground before using them in ranked matches.

A simple process is:

1. Choose one sensitivity category to change.
2. Test basic camera movement.
3. Practice close-range tracking.
4. Test recoil control with your commonly used weapons.
5. Try different scopes.
6. Make small adjustments.
7. Keep the settings for a few matches before deciding whether they work.

Changing sensitivity too frequently can make it harder to develop consistent muscle memory.

## Pro Player Settings

Professional players often use highly customized settings developed through long periods of practice.

If you are interested in a particular player's setup, check whether the settings are current before using them. Player settings can change after device upgrades, game updates, or changes in personal preference.
